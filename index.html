How We Work

Three phases designed to minimize risk, maximize ROI, and keep you in control at every stage.

I

Audit & Discovery

We analyze your existing workflows, tools, and pain points to identify where AI automation will generate the highest return — before any development begins.

II

Pilot & Validation

We build one or two targeted automations from the audit findings, deployed in your live environment, to demonstrate measurable value before full commitment.

III

Full Implementation

With a validated approach and defined scope, we build out the complete automation stack — integrated, tested, and handed over with full documentation.

Case Studies

Case Study 01 Knowledge & AI

Corpus AI — Theological Knowledge Distillation Platform

A research platform needed to make a large, multi-lingual theological corpus accessible to non-specialist users. We built an AI pipeline that ingested source documents, extracted key concepts, and surfaced contextually relevant summaries on demand — with adaptive reading levels and citation tracing throughout.

⟡ Delivered in 6 weeks, audit to launch
Case Study 02 Healthcare & Automation

MedClaim AI — Medical Billing & Denial Management

A mid-sized healthcare provider was losing significant revenue to claim denials and manual billing errors. We built an automated billing pipeline that cross-referenced patient records against ICD-10 and CPT codes, flagged high-risk submissions before they were sent, and generated denial appeal drafts in seconds — fully integrated with their existing EHR platform at zero downtime.

⟡ 34% reduction in claim denials within 60 days
